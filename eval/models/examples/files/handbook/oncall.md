# On-call (fictional)

The rotation is nine days long and hands over on Tuesdays at 10:00. Two people
are on call at once: a primary and a secondary. The primary acknowledges a page
within 5 minutes; after 15 minutes the page goes to the secondary.

New staff shadow one full rotation before they join it. An incident is closed
only after its write-up is filed, which is due within three working days.
